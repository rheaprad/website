_The Cosmic Calendar_ was created by Carl Sagan to compress the entire history of the universe into one calendar year. This allowed for a more understandable perspective on the timeline of the universe (especially to factor in that the majority of the universe’s history occurred before humans existed). 

From all the events that Sagan lists out, I picked several to depict the loose timeline through laser cut illustrations and printed text on Kozo paper. The book can be read from two different sides and rests in a clamshell box.
